Krish Ramjiyani, QA engineer

I’m a QA engineer with 1.7 years of hands-on testing across payment platforms, casino games and factory software: 7,000+ test cases and 1,000+ defects logged, retested and closed. I work remotely with a team in Austin, from India.

What I do day to day

I joined as a Quality Analyst Intern in January 2025 and became a Junior Quality Analyst in September 2025. The work is a mix of understanding the product by hand and then making quality repeatable.

  • Design and run test cases from business requirements and acceptance criteria.
  • Build reusable Playwright automation in JavaScript and widen coverage.
  • Test APIs in Postman and run load tests in JMeter. See API testing.
  • Own the defect lifecycle in JIRA, from the first report to retest and closure.
  • Join sprint triage, root-cause analysis, regression cycles and release checks.

How I approach a product

I start by reading the requirements like a skeptic, challenging assumptions and identifying risks. Then I explore real user scenarios across roles, workflows and business logic, including calculations, APIs and databases. Once a feature is stable, I automate it so regression testing is faster and more reliable. The manual side is covered in more detail on my manual testing page.
